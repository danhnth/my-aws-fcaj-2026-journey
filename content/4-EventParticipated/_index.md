During my internship, I participated in the following events. They were memorable experiences that provided new, interesting, and useful knowledge, along with gifts and wonderful moments.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)  
&emsp;**Event Name:** Agentic AI Build Week (AABW)  

&emsp;**Date & Time:** July 25, 2026  

&emsp;**Location:** AWS Event Hall - 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Summary:** A hackathon hosted by AWS focused on building Agentic AI applications on the AWS platform, culminating in a team demo day. Three products were presented: S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D (real-time crowd monitoring using computer vision and agentic AI), Signal Scout (early detection of corporate strategy-shift signals), and SA Professional Native App (an AI assistant for Solution Architects). The teams shared their architectures, operating costs, and lessons learned after 24 hours of building.
